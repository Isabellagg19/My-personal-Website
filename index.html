About me

I'm an 18-year-old Colombian girl who loves cooking (my specialty is chocolate chip cookies), spending time with my pets, and always learning something new. My two dogs and my cat are the most meaningful part of my life, they're the reason I care so deeply about animals and why I want my future to be focused on helping them and the environment. I also like to consider myself a Latin American leader. I've participated in initiatives like LALA (Latin American Leadership Academy), where I learned the foundations to start an incredible, huge path of leadership. That path eventually led me to discover organizations like Hack Club, where I started coding last year without knowing a thing about it. I began by following a tutorial and making a website about women who changed the world and it turns out I ended up falling in love with coding. Right now my biggest purpose is to keep learning about code and design, and to start a project that actually helps people become more conscious about the climate challenges we're facing and how to help improve them. I also want to encorage more young Latin American leaders to work on similar projects, to show the power and talent our continent holds.
